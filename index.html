Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how FixMate ("we", "us", "the App") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal information when you use the FixMate mobile app as a customer or as a technician. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

From all users:

  • Account information: full name, phone number, and optional email address, collected at sign-up.
  • Password, stored by our backend in hashed form — we never store or transmit your plaintext password.
  • Profile photo (optional), if you choose to add one.
  • Booking data: service requests, addresses, scheduling details, job descriptions, and any photos or videos you attach to a booking.
  • Chat messages exchanged with the other party on a booking, including any photos/voice notes sent in chat.
  • Payment and wallet data: transaction history, top-up/withdrawal amounts, and booking payment records.
  • Device push-notification token, used to deliver booking and chat notifications to your device.
  • Reviews and ratings you submit or receive.

From technicians specifically:

  • Identity verification (KYC) documents: a photo of your government-issued national ID and a selfie photo, submitted to verify your identity before you can accept jobs. These are reviewed by our admin team as part of onboarding.
  • Professional profile details: bio, years of experience, service categories, coverage city, and availability status.
  • Real-time location: while you have an active job (a booking that is Accepted or In Progress), the App streams your GPS coordinates so the customer can see your live position and estimated arrival on a map. This broadcasting stops automatically once the job is no longer active. We do not persist a location history — only your most recent position is cached briefly (currently up to 2 minutes) to support a customer's app reconnecting, and it expires automatically.

From customers specifically:

  • Saved addresses, including precise location coordinates, used to match you with nearby technicians and route them to you.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account, and authenticate you.
  • To connect customers with technicians and facilitate bookings, in-app chat, and payments.
  • To show a customer their assigned technician's live location during an active job.
  • To verify technician identity and eligibility to work on the platform (KYC review).
  • To process wallet top-ups, withdrawals, and booking payments.
  • To send booking, chat, and account notifications.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • For safety, fraud prevention, and to investigate misuse or policy violations.
  • To improve and maintain the App's reliability and features.
