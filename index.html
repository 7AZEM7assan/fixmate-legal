Privacy Policy

FixMate — Last updated: August 2026

FixMate is a home-services marketplace mobile application that connects customers with qualified technicians across Egypt (Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, New Cairo, October, and Sheikh Zayed). This privacy policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Full name, phone number, email address (optional), and authentication credentials
  • Profile Information: Profile photo (optional), service addresses, and communication preferences
  • Service Requests: Service descriptions, photos of issues, preferred dates/times, special instructions
  • Payment Information: Payment method details, processed through our backend payment system. We do not store raw card details. (Note: as of this version, payments are processed through a development/test payment provider while we finalize integration with a licensed production payment processor.)
  • Communications: In-app chat messages, voice notes, support requests, and feedback

Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: Device GPS location (with your permission) to find nearby technicians and track service arrival. You can disable location anytime in app settings or device settings.
  • Device Information: Device type, OS version, app version, device identifiers, system language
  • Usage Data: Pages accessed, features used, search queries, click patterns, crash reports, and app performance metrics
  • Network Information: IP address, connection type, mobile network operator

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and maintain your account, and process your service requests
  • Match you with nearby technicians and share booking details with them
  • Enable in-app communication (chat, voice messages, file transfer) between customers and technicians
  • Process payments through our backend payment system
  • Send booking confirmations, updates, and support responses via SMS or push notification
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Conduct KYC (Know Your Customer) verification and comply with anti-money laundering requirements
  • Improve app features, performance, and user experience through analytics
  • Respond to legal requests and comply with applicable laws

3. How We Share Your Information

Essential Sharing

  • Assigned Technician: We share your name, phone, address, and service description so they can complete your booking
  • Payment processing: payment and billing information necessary to process transactions, currently handled by a development/test payment provider ahead of production launch
  • OTP delivery: your phone number and one-time login codes are currently generated and logged server-side during development, ahead of integrating a production SMS delivery provider
  • File storage: photos and documents you upload — including technician KYC documents — are currently stored on our backend server's own disk, ahead of migrating to dedicated cloud object storage before production launch
  • Maps Service (Google Maps API): location queries for distance and ETA calculations

We Do NOT Sell Your Data

FixMate does not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing or commercial purposes. We do not use your data for behavioral advertising or data brokerage.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose personal data if required by law (court order, subpoena, government request) or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect safety, enforce our Terms of Service, prevent fraud, or comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Retention

Data Category Retention Period
Account information While account is active + 2 years after deletion
Booking & service history While account is active + 5 years after completion
Payment records While account is active + 5 years (required for tax compliance)
Chat & messages While account is active + 6 months after booking
Photos & voice messages While account is active + 2 years
Location data (real-time tracking) During booking only; deleted after completion
Analytics & usage logs 18 months
Device & IP logs 90 days

You can request deletion of your account and associated data anytime through the app's Profile settings. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where legal obligations require retention.

5. Your Rights & Choices

Account Controls

  • Edit your profile (name, email, address, phone)
  • Enable/disable location, camera, microphone, photo library permissions in app settings
  • Opt out of promotional messages (Settings → Preferences → Unsubscribe)
  • Request account deletion (Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account)

Location Permissions

You can disable location tracking anytime:

  • In FixMate: Settings → Permissions → Location (toggle off)
  • On your device: iOS Settings → Privacy → Location Services, or Android Settings → Apps → Permissions → Location
  • Real-time tracking during service delivery can be disabled via the booking screen
  • If location is disabled, you can still use FixMate by manually entering your address

Data Access & Deletion

To request access, deletion, or correction of your personal data:

  • Email: zuma.eg44@gmail.com
  • In-app: Profile → Help & Support → Privacy Request

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

FixMate is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we learn that we have collected data from a child under 13 without proper consent, we will delete that data immediately.

For users aged 13–17, parents and guardians have the right to request access to their child's data, require account deletion, or restrict their child's use of the app. If you are a parent concerned about your child's use of FixMate, contact us at zuma.eg44@gmail.com.

7. Security

FixMate uses industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications use TLS/SSL encryption
  • Authentication: Phone number + one-time code for login; passwords hashed, never stored in plaintext
  • Access Controls: Access to sensitive data (such as KYC documents) is restricted on a need-to-know basis
  • Incident Response: We have a data breach response plan and will notify affected users as required by law if a breach occurs

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately at zuma.eg44@gmail.com.

By using FixMate, you consent to the transfer of your personal data to Egypt and other countries for processing as described in this policy.
